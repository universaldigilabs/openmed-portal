1. Executive Summary & Platform Overview

Med Vantage connects international patients with accredited cross-border healthcare providers (Apollo, Fortis, Bumrungrad, Acıbadem, Gleneagles, Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, Médica Sur, Quirónsalud). The platform provides end-to-end pricing transparency, multi-tier accreditation verification (JCI & NABH), remote super-specialist second opinions, and full travel coordination (hotel, visa, insurance, VIP transfers, and medical translators).

3. Core Functional Pillars

🏥 Hospital & Clinic Verification

Verified accreditations (JCI & NABH). Complete multi-filter directory by country, specialty, and technology across 8 flagship international hospitals.

📊 Cost Transparency & Savings

Interactive benchmark engine comparing US, UK, India, Thailand, Turkey, Mexico, and Spain. Visual indicators highlight 60%–85% cost savings in India.

🩺 Remote Second Opinions

48-hour tele-triage with world-renowned surgical specialists. 100% of remote consultation fee credited back upon hospital admission.

✈️ Travel, Visa, & Insurance Hub

End-to-end concierge covering medical visas (e-Visa), sanitized recovery hotels/serviced apartments, cashless insurance TPA, and native interpreters.

4. Patient Concierge & Triage Workflows

  • Patient Intake Form (/intake): Multi-step interactive medical inquiry workflow capturing medical diagnostics, preferred hospital/country, plus explicit selections for Hotel Booking Assistance (Serviced Apartment, 4/5-Star Hotel, Recovery Suite), Medical Visa Attendant Count, and Health Insurance Pre-Authorization.
  • Coordinator Triage Console (/coordinator): Live operations dashboard for care managers featuring instant assistance filtering (Hotel, Visa, Insurance), patient audit log, and one-click quote generation.
